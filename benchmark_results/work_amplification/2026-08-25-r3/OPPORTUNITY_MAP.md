## Immediate research sequence

1. Turn derived-carrier reuse into an audit: inventory immutable derived state,
   owner identity, generation invalidation, retained bytes, and repeated build
   counts before proposing a shared cache abstraction.
2. Write a GIS/physical-shape design for equal-radius stroke coverage with two
   structurally different algorithms: tiled boundary arrangement and
   segment/arc event reduction. Reject either before kernel work if peak-live
   or topology reconstruction cannot fit the target envelope.
3. Keep Q12 and broader parent-aware reductions queued until the first two
   falsifiers produce current complete-workflow evidence.

The device-planning program stays archived. None of these findings justifies a
device-name table or a cross-library automatic planner; selection remains
shape- and evidence-derived.
